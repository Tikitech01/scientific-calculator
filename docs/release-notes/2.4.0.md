A free, offline scientific calculator for engineering students — graphing, equations, matrices,
complex numbers, calculus, statistics, finance and a programmer mode in one fast native app.

## What's new in 2.4.0

- **Smooth animation** everywhere outside the graph, with Off / Slow / Normal / Fast in Settings
- **Redesigned Settings and Input guide**: grouped, cleaner, easier to scan
- **Faster**: expensive results are only recomputed when their inputs change
- **Help menu**: Check for Updates and Send Feedback
- Fix: the highlight behind the selected option now always sits under its label

Recent highlights: Tukey HSD and two-way ANOVA, non-parametric tests, graph sliders and
inequalities, polar complex numbers (`5∠30`), inline unit conversion (`5 km to m`) and a Finance
mode (EMI, NPV/IRR, TVM). Full history in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Tikitech01/scientific-calculator/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
