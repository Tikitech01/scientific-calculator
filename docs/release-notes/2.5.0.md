A free, offline scientific calculator for engineering students — graphing, equations, matrices,
complex numbers, calculus, statistics, finance and a programmer mode in one fast native app.

## What's new in 2.5.0

Built for circuits and physics homework:

- **Physical constants** by name: `kB·300`, `NA·qe`, `1/√(μ0·ε0)`. 2nd + αβγ shows them on the keys.
- **SI prefixes** typed as on the schematic: `4.7kΩ ∥ 10kΩ`, `220µF`, `5mA·2kΩ`, `1/(2π·1kHz·100nF)`
- **Eng display**: results as `3.197k`, `2.2m`, `4.7µ`
- **Parallel operator** `∥` (2nd + %, or type `||`), also for complex impedances
- **dB**: `dB(2)` = 3.01, `dBv(10)` = 20, `invdB`, `invdBv`
- **Limits and series**: `limit(sin(x)/x, x, 0)`, `limit((1+1/x)^x, x, inf)`, `taylor(e^x, x, 0, 4)`
- **Eigenvalues and eigenvectors**: `eig(MatA)`, `eigvec(MatA)`, with buttons in Matrix mode

Full history in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Tikitech01/scientific-calculator/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
